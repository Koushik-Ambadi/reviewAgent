Future direction · evidence first

From repository review to engineering intelligence

Connect the embedded modules and their code, requirements, tests, builds, findings, and delivery history. The graph is a shared evidence model; developers, testers, managers, product owners, auditors, and customers get focused views for the questions they need to answer.

How to read this roadmapEvery capability below is planned or exploratory. Compiler facts, test observations, imported records, human decisions, and model suggestions must remain distinguishable and traceable to their source.

1 · Build the shared evidence foundation

Roadmap gates G02, G08, G09 and G12

Planned · G08

Cross-module code graph

Move from file-scoped symbol lists to versioned, linked program facts across all modules, with exact source and configuration provenance.

  • Stable symbol identity; declarations, definitions and references
  • Include, interface, global-state and direct caller/callee edges
  • Per-function statements, control-flow and selected data-flow
  • Generated, third-party, indirect-call and parse-coverage labels
Planned · G02 / G12

Reproducible evidence and audit history

Make each graph and decision reproducible for a specific source revision, policy, build configuration and toolchain.

  • Versioned run manifests, tool versions, input/output hashes
  • Artifact integrity and provenance checks, with signature validation when supported
  • Inventory reconciliation and reviewer/approver decisions
  • Finding, deviation and approval links with actor and policy history
  • Explicit partial, skipped, uncertain and unavailable evidence
Planned · G09

Rule and compliance evidence

Grow deterministic checks from naming into graph-aware rules while retaining rule scope, applicability, exceptions and review evidence.

  • Function metrics, recursion and architecture boundaries
  • Static-analysis imports with rule and source locations
  • MISRA, AUTOSAR and project-rule evidence by version
  • Deviation records and manual review where automation cannot decide
Exploration

Analysis engine and format adapters

Keep the production compiler authoritative. Evaluate graph-analysis engines and standard interchange formats against representative embedded C before selecting adapters.

  • Compare Clang/LibTooling with code-property-graph tooling such as Joern
  • Import findings through SARIF; preserve GHS diagnostics and build evidence
  • Evaluate ReqIF requirements links and ASAM XIL test-bench connections
  • Record licensing, configuration fidelity, unresolved symbols and coverage

2 · Connect engineering work to verification and delivery

Future gates after provenance and graph foundations

Future · G13

Requirements-to-release traceability

Link customer and software requirements to modules, interfaces, code, findings, test cases, execution results and release artifacts.

  • Show requirements implemented, unverified, blocked or missing evidence
  • Attach build, binary, map, calibration and configuration identities
  • Export a reviewable evidence package for client and auditor handoff
Future · G14

Change impact, debugging and test selection

Compare graph snapshots for a commit or PR, connect incidents to their tested baseline, and explain affected modules, requirements, risks and tests.

  • Rank direct and indirect impact with uncertainty visible
  • Recommend regression tests across MIL, SIL and HIL
  • Trace observed signals and logs to relevant writers, call paths and recent changes
  • Surface coupling, complexity and performance hotspots with measurement evidence
  • Link test results, coverage and defects to the exact revision/configuration
Planned · G11

Governed PR, tickets and approvals

Turn findings and impact evidence into proposed work, with authorized owners, external IDs, review history and explicit approval transitions.

  • Provider-backed issue, source-control and CI integrations
  • Idempotent actions, role checks and durable audit events
  • Preview-only manager controls stay labeled until writes are real
Planned · G11

Portfolio and progress views

Give managers and product owners a cross-run view of evidence completeness and delivery risk rather than activity counts alone.

  • Compare modules, profiles, revisions and release configurations
  • Track requirement, review, test and deviation states
  • Show blockers, trends, ownership and confidence with drill-down evidence

3 · Add assistance after the evidence is dependable

Future gate G15 · opt-in and evaluated

Future · G15

Neuro-symbolic engineering assistant

Pair deterministic graph queries, rule checks and test evidence with optional language-model synthesis and retrieval over approved engineering records.

  • Explain a finding or failure through its code and evidence paths
  • Draft review notes, test plans and investigation steps for human review
  • Keep compiler facts, observed results, document claims and model suggestions visibly distinct
Research · gated by history

Risk and failure prediction

Explore statistical or graph-learning models only after enough labeled changes, failures and test outcomes exist to compare against a simple baseline.

  • Potential heads: defect risk, affected tests, recurring failures and review priority
  • Evaluate by module, configuration, time period and false-negative cost
  • Show calibrated uncertainty; predictions never become compliance verdicts

First validation slice

Start with the shunt module: generate a deterministic graph, review one real change, trace its callers and affected modules, connect the available tests and findings, and compare the result with an engineer’s hand-checked impact analysis. Expand to all ten modules only after identity, coverage and provenance hold up.
